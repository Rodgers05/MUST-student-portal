How to Apply

Follow these general steps when preparing your application to MUST.

1

Create an Account

Select Create Account and provide your name, email address, phone number and a secure password.

2

Sign In

Return to the sign-in page and access your account using your registered email address and password.

3

Complete the Application Form

Provide your personal information, academic background and programme choices accurately.

4

Prepare Required Documents

Prepare your academic documents and any supporting documents required for your application category.

5

Review and Submit

Check all entered information carefully and follow the official instructions for fees and final submission.

Important Information

Entry requirements, application deadlines, fees and required documents may change. Confirm current details through MUST's official website before applying.
